About Us

The Story Behind ProjectX HUB

A digital services studio built on one simple belief — good ideas deserve great execution.

ProjectX HUB was founded by Prince Verma with a clear goal: to give individuals and small businesses access to the same quality of digital work that large agencies offer — without the overhead, the long timelines, or the confusing jargon.

What started as a single freelancer helping local businesses with websites and marketing has grown into a full-service digital studio covering web development, AI strategy, data analysis, digital marketing, social media management, performance marketing, academic projects, and business research.

Every project that comes through ProjectX HUB is handled with the same principle: understand the real problem first, then build something that actually solves it — not just something that looks good on the surface.

Alongside client work, ProjectX HUB is also building its own ecosystem of products, including ProjectX AI Studio for AI-powered project workflows, and a growing team of specialists who share the same commitment to quality and honest communication.
