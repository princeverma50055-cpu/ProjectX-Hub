Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026. Discover how ProjectX Hub collects, utilizes, and safeguards your data across our digital platforms.

1. Introduction & Scope

ProjectX Hub ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we gather, utilize, disclose, and secure information when you visit projectxhub.in or interact with our digital services.

By accessing our website, you consent to the data practices described within this policy document.

2. Information We Collect

Voluntary Information: When you submit project requests via our "Start a Project" application form or contact portals, we collect identifying details such as your full name, email address, phone number, company name, and specific project goals.

Automatic Analytics Data: Like most modern platforms, our servers automatically log technical information including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and general session duration to help us optimize server performance.

3. Use of Cookies

We deploy standard browser cookies to personalize your browsing experience, remember session preferences, and analyze aggregate site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings, though certain interactive features of our platform may become restricted as a result.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Our website may display advertisements served via Google AdSense. Google utilizes cookies (such as the DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their prior visits to this website and other sites across the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. For further details on how Google handles advertising data, please review Google’s Partner Policy.

5. How We Use Your Data

All information submitted through our contact and application forms is utilized strictly for the following purposes:

  • Responding directly to project enquiries and custom development consultations.
  • Delivering requested digital roadmaps, proposals, and updates.
  • Improving website usability, navigation speed, and user experience.
  • Complying with necessary legal obligations and resolving contractual disputes.

We never sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third-party marketing brokers.

6. Data Security Measures

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures—including SSL encryption and secure cloud environments—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
