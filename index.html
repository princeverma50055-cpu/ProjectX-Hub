Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting a project.

How do I get started with a project? +
Fill out the application form below with your project details, or reach out directly via WhatsApp or email. Prince will review your requirements and get back to you within 24 hours to discuss scope, timeline, and pricing.
What is the typical project timeline? +
Simple projects (landing pages, basic reports) usually take 3-5 days. Larger projects like full websites, AI automation, or ongoing marketing typically take 1-3 weeks depending on scope and revisions.
How does payment work? +
Payment terms are discussed after the initial scope call — typically a partial advance to begin work, with the remaining balance on delivery. Details are shared once your project requirements are confirmed.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes — every package includes a set number of revision rounds so the final result matches your vision. Additional revisions beyond the included rounds can be arranged if needed.
Can you handle academic or research projects too? +
Yes — alongside commercial digital services, ProjectX Hub also supports academic projects, business research, and data analysis assignments with clear documentation and timely delivery.
Is my project idea kept confidential? +
Absolutely. All project details shared with ProjectX Hub are treated as confidential and used only for the purpose of delivering your project.
